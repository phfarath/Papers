On first exposure all methods are alike (nothing is consolidated yet); on re-exposure the astrocyte restores the context's consolidated trace, cutting the re-adaptation error roughly in half, while a shared, ungated trace (no context signal) loses the benefit — the paper's own caveat that the network receives contextual information.

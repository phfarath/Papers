# RESULTS — p21 Building Transformers from Neurons and Astrocytes

Generated by `python -m papers.p21_astrocyte_transformer.run --write-results` (fixed seed, offline, numpy only). Demonstrates the proposed computational correspondence — a mechanism result, not a benchmark.

Random q, K, V (d=8, 10 keys, 6 values); the ODE integrates fast neuron units holding scores s=q·k/√d plus a slow astrocyte pooling total activity for divisive normalization. Errors = max |output − softmax(s)·V| over 8 seeds.

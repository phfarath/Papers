## Leitura / reading

- NN stalls on some XOR/parity3 seeds (saturated sigmoids + few hidden units); the astrocyte kick rescues part of them — success ↑ without changing the architecture.
- On easy tasks (sel_and, sparse_parity) the glia is ~neutral — matches the paper: the benefit is task-dependent, not uniform.
- O astrócito não melhora tudo — o ganho aparece onde a GD pura estagna, como no paper.

Hopfield collapses beyond ~0.2·N stored patterns while astrocyte-mediated higher-order models keep retrieving at several-fold higher load, and the gap widens with N — the mechanism behind the paper's supralinear capacity claim. The β-sweep shows the soft softmax readout approaching the hard winner-take-all limit (the Transformer correspondence).
